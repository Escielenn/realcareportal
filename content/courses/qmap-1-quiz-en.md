Real Care, Inc. — QMAP Training Part 1 (Units 1 & 2) Quiz (English)

Instructions: Select the best answer for each question.

1. What does QMAP stand for?

A) Qualified Medical Assessment Professional
B) Qualified Medication Administration Person
C) Quality Medication Approval Program
D) Qualified Medicine and Pharmacy

2. What is the minimum passing score on the QMAP written exam?

A) 70%
B) 75%
C) 80%
D) 85%

3. Which of the following is TRUE about completing the QMAP course?

A) It certifies you as a licensed medication aide
B) It authorizes you to make clinical judgments about a client's medications
C) It qualifies you to administer medications according to written physician's orders, but does not certify or license you
D) It allows you to administer medications by injection

4. What is the difference between "monitoring" and "administering" medication?

A) There is no difference
B) Monitoring involves reminding and observing the client; administering involves directly assisting with ingestion, application, inhalation, or insertion

6. Which of the following is one of the Seven Rights of Medication Administration covered in this course?

B) Right to refuse
C) Right to skip documentation
D) Right to adjust the dose without a physician's order

A) Injection, ingestion, inhalation, insertion
B) Ingestion, application, inhalation, insertion
C) Ingestion, application, injection, insertion
D) Oral, injection, topical, inhalation

B) Always measure using the metric system and appropriate measuring devices

A) Any medication that requires a prescription
B) A drug subject to restrictions with potential for addiction, classified into five schedules
C) An over-the-counter drug with side effects
D) A medication that must be stored in a refrigerator

A) Administer the medication and ask questions later
B) Skip the medication entirely and do not document it
C) Review the label, contact the pharmacist or nurse for clarification, and do not administer until the question is resolved
D) Ask another QMAP to make the decision for you

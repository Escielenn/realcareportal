QMAP Part 2 — Units 3, 4 & 5 — Quiz

Instructions: Choose the best answer for each question.

﻿


1. Which of the following is NOT one of the six required parts of a medication order?

A. Client's first and last name
B. Medication name
C. Client's insurance information
D. Physician/provider signature

﻿


2. A medication "dose" has three parts. Which of the following correctly identifies those three parts?

A. Size, frequency, and duration
B. Strength, route, and time
C. Medication, dose, and route
D. Tablets, milligrams, and milliliters

﻿


3. Which of the following measurement equivalents is correct?

A. 1 teaspoon = 15 mL
B. 1 tablespoon = 5 mL
C. 2 tablespoons = 30 mL = 1 ounce
D. 1 cc = 5 mL

﻿


4. As a QMAP, which type of medication order are you allowed to accept?

A. A verbal order given by a physician over the phone
B. A written physician's order
C. A faxed order from a pharmacist that is not a copy of a signed physician order
D. A verbal "resume previous orders" instruction after a hospital discharge

﻿


5. What does the medical abbreviation "prn" mean?

A. By mouth
B. As needed
C. At bedtime
D. Three times a day

﻿


6. What should you do if you make a documentation error on the MAR?

A. Use white-out to cover the mistake and write the correct entry
B. Erase the mistake and write the correct entry in pencil
C. Draw a single line through the mistake, initial and date it, and explain on the back of the MAR
D. Throw away the MAR and start a new one

﻿


7. When should you document medication administration on the MAR?

A. Before administering the medication, so you don't forget
B. Immediately after giving or monitoring the medication
C. At the end of your shift, all at once
D. The following day during a quiet moment

﻿


8. How should you document a PRN medication that was administered?

A. Just initial the box; no further documentation is needed
B. Initial the appropriate box and document the time, dose, and reason on the reverse side of the MAR
C. Document only on the back of the MAR, with no entry in the date box
D. Wait 24 hours, then document

﻿


9. Which of the following should NOT be placed in a Medication Reminder Box (MRB)?

A. A routinely scheduled tablet
B. A routinely scheduled capsule
C. A controlled substance
D. A daily multivitamin tablet

﻿


10. Before filling an MRB, you must cross-check the MRB label against which of the following?

A. Only the medication bottle
B. Only the MAR
C. Only the physician's order
D. The physician's order, the MAR, AND the pharmacist-prepared medication bottle

﻿

Answer Key

C — A medication order requires the client's name, medication, dose, route, date, and physician/provider signature. Insurance information is not a required part of the order.

A — A dose has three parts: size (the amount given at one time), frequency (how often it's given), and duration (how long it's taken).

C — 2 tablespoons = 30 mL = 1 ounce. (1 tsp = 5 mL; 1 Tbsp = 15 mL = ½ oz; 1 cc = 1 mL.)

B — QMAPs may only act on a written physician's order. Verbal/phone orders are not accepted, faxes from a pharmacist are not accepted unless they're a copy of a signed physician order, and "resume previous orders" is not acceptable after a hospital stay.

B — "prn" means as needed.

C — Draw a single line through the mistaken entry, initial and date it, and explain on the back of the MAR. Never use white-out, pencil, or attempt to erase.

B — Always document immediately after giving or monitoring a medication, never before.

B — Initial the appropriate box on the MAR and document the time, dose, and reason why the PRN was administered on the reverse side (or other designated area) of the MAR. Then follow up within 30–60 minutes and document the client's status.

C — Controlled substances cannot be placed in an MRB. Other items that cannot go in an MRB include PRN medications, liquid medications, powders, inhalers, ointments and creams, and medications with special instructions.

D — The MRB label must be cross-checked against all three: the physician's order, the MAR, and the pharmacist-prepared medication bottle. Any discrepancy must be resolved before filling.

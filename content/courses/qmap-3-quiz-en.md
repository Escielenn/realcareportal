QMAP Training Part 3 — Quiz

Covers Units 6, 7, and 8
Real Care, Inc.

﻿


1. According to the Seven Rights of medication administration, the "right time" means administering medication within what window of the scheduled time stated in the doctor's order?

A) 15 minutes before to 15 minutes after

B) 30 minutes before to 30 minutes after

C) 1 hour before to 1 hour after

D) Any time during the scheduled shift

﻿


2. When do standard precautions apply?

A) Only with clients known to have infectious diseases

B) Only when there is visible blood

C) For all clients at all times in all contacts

D) Only when administering injections

﻿


3. What is the single most important measure to prevent contamination and the spread of infection?

A) Wearing gloves

B) Hand washing

C) Wearing a mask

D) Disinfecting surfaces

﻿


4. How many times must you cross-check the medication label against the MAR and physician's order during administration?

A) Once

B) Twice

C) Three times

D) Four times

﻿


5. When administering nitroglycerin SL tablets to a client experiencing chest pain, the client should be instructed to:

A) Lie down flat

B) Sit down

C) Walk around to improve circulation

D) Stand with arms raised

﻿


6. When applying a transdermal patch, which of the following must you do?

A) Apply it to the same site each time for consistency

B) Cut the patch in half if a smaller dose is needed

C) Write your initials, date, and time on the patch after applying

D) Leave the old patch in place to ensure continuous dosing

﻿


7. When inserting a rectal suppository, the client should be positioned:

A) Lying on their right side

B) Lying on their left side

C) Standing up

D) Sitting upright in a chair

﻿


8. After administering medication to a client, what must you do?

A) Wait until the end of your shift to document on the MAR

B) Document each medication on the MAR immediately after administration

C) Document only PRN medications on the MAR

D) Only document if the client refused the medication

﻿


9. Which of the following best defines a medication error?

A) Any time medication is given a few minutes late

B) Medication administered contrary to a physician's order that either causes or has the potential to cause harm

C) Only errors that result in hospitalization

D) Forgetting to wear gloves during administration

﻿

A) On the kitchen counter for easy access

B) In a single locked cabinet with no count required

C) Double-locked, counted, and signed for using a second person for verification

D) With other prescription medications in the medication reminder box

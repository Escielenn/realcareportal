Real Care, Inc. — Injection Administration Quiz (English)

Instructions: Select the best answer for each question.

﻿


1. Injection administration may only be performed when which conditions are met?

A) The caregiver has watched an online video and feels confident
B) A licensed Real Care nurse delegates the task, training is provided, and competency is verified
C) The individual's family gives verbal permission
D) The caregiver has prior personal experience giving injections

﻿


2. What are the two types of injections covered in this training?

A) Intravenous (IV) and Subcutaneous (SubQ)
B) Subcutaneous (SubQ) and Intramuscular (IM)
C) Intramuscular (IM) and Intravenous (IV)
D) Topical and Subcutaneous (SubQ)

﻿


3. How many "Rights" of Medication Administration must be verified before every injection?

A) 3
B) 5
C) 7
D) 10

﻿


4. Which of the following is a reason to NOT administer an injection?

A) The individual is nervous about the injection
B) The medication is expired
C) It is the individual's first time receiving an injection
D) The injection is scheduled for the afternoon

﻿


5. What is the FIRST step in infection control before administering an injection?

A) Put on gloves
B) Open the needle packaging
C) Wash your hands
D) Clean the injection site

﻿


6. After administering an injection, used needles should be:

A) Recapped and placed in regular trash
B) Rinsed and returned to the pharmacy
C) Disposed of immediately in an approved sharps container
D) Placed in a sealed plastic bag

﻿


7. Which of the following is a sign of a SEVERE allergic reaction?

A) Mild soreness at the injection site
B) Minor redness
C) Swelling of the lips, face, or throat
D) A small bruise

﻿


8. What should you do if you observe signs of a severe allergic reaction?

A) Wait to see if symptoms improve on their own
B) Stop the procedure, notify the nurse, follow emergency protocol, and call 911 if life-threatening
C) Give a second dose of the medication
D) Apply ice to the injection site and continue monitoring

﻿


9. Which of the following must be documented on the MAR immediately after an injection?

A) Only the medication name and date
B) Medication name, dose, route, injection site, date, time, and any reactions
C) Only whether the individual complained of pain
D) The brand name of the syringe used

﻿


10. How often is injection competency reassessed?

A) Only once, during initial training
B) Every 5 years
C) Annually, when care plans change, or if incidents occur
D) Only if the provider requests it

﻿

C — 7

C — Wash your hands

C — Swelling of the lips, face, or throat

## Not converted

- **ATTENDANCE-RECORD_gravityforms-export.json**: Older version of the Medicaid Residential Attendance Record (Dec 2025 export used).
- **RESIDENTIAL-TRACKING-FORM_full-month_gravityforms-export.json**: Superseded by the Grid List version (1,342 fields; the grid version covers the same month in 21).
- **SCC-LOG-NOTES-ONLY_gravityforms-export.json**: Older version of SCC Log Notes (Dec 2025 export used).
- **SCC-TIMESHEETS-ONLY_gravityforms-export.json**: Older version of the DSP/SCC timesheet (Dec 2025 export used).
- **MENU-TRACKING_gravityforms-export.json**: Older version of Menu Tracking (Dec 2025 export used).

## Needs a person to check

- RESIDENTIAL-TRACKING_Grid-List_autoadd_gravityforms-export.json: day-by-day lists became month grids with codes Y/N/R; 'Other (desc)' columns became a notes box. R is read as 'Refused' (from the form's note).

## Removed during conversion

- **DSP/SCC Provider Timesheet**: Form Header (decoration); Total Direct Care Hours (Auto-Calculated) (replaced by a native grid); Timesheet Data (Hidden - Auto-Populated) (replaced by a native grid); Submission Info (decoration)
- **SCC Log Notes**: Form Header (decoration)
- **Medicaid Residential Attendance Record**: Form Header (decoration); Total # of days residential services were provided to individual named above by the caregiver (replaced by a native grid); Total # of trips that were provided to individual named above by the caregiver (replaced by a native grid); Submission Info (decoration)
- **Standard Goal Tracking**: Form Header (decoration)
- **NMT Trips Tracking**: Trips by Day (auto-fills 31 rows) (replaced by a native grid); Monthly Total Trips (auto) (the portal counts this itself)
- **Menu Tracking**: Form Header (decoration); Menu Data (Hidden - Auto-Populated) (replaced by a native grid); Submission Info (decoration)

## Spanish

The intranet forms are English only. 314 labels and notes show English in the Spanish view until they are translated. Instructions have a short Spanish version.
